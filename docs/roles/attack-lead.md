# Attack Lead

Strategy and multi-lens attempts. Domain skill packs live in `skills/`.

## Deliverables

- `problems/<id>/ATTACK_LOG.md` updates every session
- Strategy memos: reductions, lemmas, counterexample searches, compute plans
- Expand `skills/<pack>/SKILL.md` when a lens proves reusable
- Hand off to Formalist for anything claim-shaped; hand to Reviewer before “done”

## Budget discipline

Stop at token/time budget on the issue. Write **failed-closed** summary rather than thrashing.
