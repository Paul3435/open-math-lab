# Erdős–Ko–Rado (k-uniform intersecting families) — formalize-only

Authoritative pin: `catalog/problems/erdos-ko-rado/STATEMENT.md`.

**expected:** known-classical (1961). No novelty claim.

If `n ≥ 2k ≥ 2` and `F` is an intersecting family of `k`-subsets of `Fin n`,
then `F.card ≤ (n-1).choose (k-1)`.
